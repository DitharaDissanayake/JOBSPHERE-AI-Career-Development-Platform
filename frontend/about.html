About JOBSPHERE

A smarter, more personal way to prepare for the career you want.

JOBSPHERE is an AI-assisted career development platform designed to help users understand their strengths, explore realistic career directions and prepare for employment with practical guidance.

Why JOBSPHERE?

Career decisions often require more than a list of job titles. Users need a clear understanding of their current skills, realistic options, gaps to close and how to present themselves confidently.

Personalized: recommendations use the user's own profile and career context.
Practical: outputs focus on roadmaps, answer structures, preparation and next actions.
Connected: career guidance, resume analysis and interview preparation work together.
Continuous: useful sessions and resources remain available in the user's account.

Designed around the user

The platform is built around a simple idea: understand the person first, then provide guidance that fits the person. From profile-based learning and secure course payments to industry conversations through WhatsApp, each feature is connected to the learner's account.

Our approach

Personal context + AI + practical learning + industry connection.
