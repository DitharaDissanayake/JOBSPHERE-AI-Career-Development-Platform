How to use JOBSPHERE

Follow these simple procedures to understand what each tool does, what information it uses and what output you should expect.

1. My Profile

Your profile is the foundation for personalization.

  1. Open My Profile.
  2. Enter education, skills, soft skills, experience, projects, career interest and career goal.
  3. Save the profile.
  4. Keep it updated when your skills or goals change.
Output: your profile becomes available to the AI career and interview tools.

2. AI Career Advisor

Use this after completing your profile.

  1. Open AI Career Advisor.
  2. Run the analysis.
  3. Review career paths, match score, strengths, weaknesses and skill gaps.
  4. Follow the development roadmap.
Output: personalized career analysis saved to your account.

3. Resume Analyzer

Upload your PDF/DOC/DOCX resume.

  1. Choose your resume file.
  2. Upload and analyze.
  3. Review skills, strengths, gaps and recommendations.
  4. Update your resume and analyze again when needed.
Output: AI resume analysis connected to your profile.

4. Interview Simulator

Practice for a specific category and job position.

  1. Open Interview Simulator.
  2. Leave filters empty for profile-based recommendations, or enter a category and position.
  3. Generate the session.
  4. Read each question, why it is asked, ideal answer and tips.
Output: 10 personalized questions and answer guidance saved in history.

5. Recommended Videos & Podcasts

Use the learning resources after generating an interview session.

  1. Open the Recommended Resources section.
  2. Select a topic relevant to your target role.
  3. Watch/listen using the resource link.
  4. Return later to view your previously saved recommendations.
Output: career and interview learning resources linked to your account.

6. Feedback & Contact Admin

Use Feedback for product improvements and Contact Admin for support.

  1. Choose the relevant category.
  2. Give a clear rating or message.
  3. Submit the form.
  4. Check your email if an administrator replies.
Output: your message is stored for JOBSPHERE administration.

7. Personalized Dashboard

Your dashboard brings your career activity and AI insights together in one place.

  1. Complete My Profile for personalization.
  2. Review career match, technical level and growth insights.
  3. Check strengths, focus areas and next actions.
  4. Continue directly to Career Advisor, Resume Analyzer, Courses, Settings or AI Chat.
Output: one personalized career workspace based on your account.

8. JOBSPHERE AI Chat

Chat with your personal AI assistant for general questions, learning, coding, writing, career help and more.

  1. Open AI Chat from the dashboard or sidebar.
  2. Start a new conversation and ask your question in natural language.
  3. Continue the same conversation with follow-up questions.
  4. Use Recent Conversations to reopen previous chats linked to your JOBSPHERE account.
Output: instant AI answers with conversation history saved privately to your user account.

9. Resume Builder

Create and refine a professional resume using the information in your career workspace.

  1. Open Resume Builder.
  2. Enter or review your professional details.
  3. Build your resume and check the final structure.
  4. Use Resume Analyzer to identify improvements when needed.
Output: a structured professional resume ready for further editing.

10. Courses & Certificates

Learn practical, job-ready skills through short courses and complete assessments.

  1. Browse available courses by category.
  2. Read the introduction, modules, outcomes and industry application.
  3. Complete the course assessment.
  4. Review your result and access your certificate when eligible.
Output: tracked learning progress, assessment results and course certificates.

11. Settings & Preferences

Control the experience and notifications connected to your JOBSPHERE account.

  1. Open Settings from the sidebar.
  2. Manage email notifications, course reminders and career recommendations.
  3. Choose profile visibility, theme and language preferences.
  4. Save your changes.
Output: your selected JOBSPHERE account preferences are saved to your profile.
